# Community state and `#live` (frontend)

Developer reference for the frontend side of Meshloom Community: the Community
store and settings, the Live tab and its region filter. The invariants are
summarised in `frontend/AGENTS.md`; the backend side is in
`docs/dev/community-client.md`. Check everything here against `frontend/src/`.

## Community state
`CommunityStatus` (`GET /api/community`) carries `enabled`, `iata`, `publisher_configured`, `publisher_connected` and `state` (`off` | `active` | `waiting_for_position`, optional for an older backend), `banned` (optional) and `opt_out_pending` only: the Community addresses are fixed in the backend and never shown or sent, and `CommunityUpdate` is `{enabled?, iata?, opt_out_reason?}` (`opt_out_reason` only with `enabled: false`). When Community banned this radio key (`banned: true`, `docs/dev/community-client.md`, "Banned key"), `SettingsCommunitySection` shows a calm notice (`data-testid="community-banned"`, `role="status"`, `settings.community.banned.title` / `.body`, with the `privacy@meshloom.app` link the exit dialog also gives), on or off; nothing else changes (Leave and the IATA form stay). A join or IATA bind refused for the ban answers 403 `community_banned`, translated by `errors.community_banned`. After an opt-out Community did not confirm, `CommunityStatus.opt_out_pending` (`{requested_at, reason}`, while off) makes `SettingsCommunitySection` show "Deletion not confirmed" (`data-testid="community-opt-out-pending"`) with "Retry" (`api.retryCommunityOptOut()`, `POST /api/community/opt-out/retry`; manual only, the backend never retries on its own; `errors.opt_out_other_radio`, `errors.opt_out_key_unknown` and `errors.no_pending_opt_out` translate its 409s). A new install starts with Community off (`settings.community.intro` says so; the setup banner offers to join). `stores/communityStore.ts` is the only source of "is Community on" (`useCommunityEnabled()`, `useCommunityStatus()`). Its `enabled` means "the Community features may run" (`communityStatusActive()`): on **and not waiting for a position**, so every surface below treats `waiting_for_position` as off. Only `SettingsCommunitySection` reads `status.enabled` (the opt-in) to keep the Leave button and the IATA form; while waiting it asks no stats, and says why (`settings.community.waiting.settingsNote`). `AppShell` then shows `CommunityWaitingBanner` on every page (settings included): a banner in a named region (`<section aria-label>`, not a live region: it is mounted with its content and holds buttons) with "Choose my position" (`useCommunityPositionPicker` in `components/CommunityPositionPicker.tsx`: the lazy `LocationPickerModal`, then `PATCH /api/radio/config {lat, lon}`; the backend ends the wait once the bind carrying that position succeeds, about 5 s later, which the `settings.community.waiting.positionSaved` toast announces) and "Learn more" (a dialog that explains, with "Leave Community" and "Choose my position"). Leaving there and in the settings goes through `hooks/useCommunityLeave.tsx`: `requestLeave()` opens `CommunityLeaveDialog` (rendered by the caller as `leaveDialog`), which says what Community keeps (observations under the radio's name), deletes (the position) and cannot hide (a position the adverts broadcast), gives `privacy@meshloom.app` for a full deletion, and offers an optional reason (`settings.community.optOut.*`); only its confirm button sends `PATCH {enabled:false, opt_out_reason?}`. An answer with `opt_out_pending` gives `settings.community.optOut.notConfirmedToast` instead of `settings.community.left`. Strings live under `settings.community.waiting.*` in the base files and the `a` and `c` slices. App settings seed it on startup from `directory_available`; `GET`/`PATCH /api/community` answers replace the seed (`setCommunityStatus`, `refreshCommunityStatus`); every `community_live` WS status triggers a re-read (concurrent re-reads share one request), and so does a WS reconnect, so other tabs follow an opt-out, an opt-in and an IATA change. Nothing keeps a copy: `SettingsCommunitySection` reads its status from the store, loads it with `loadCommunityStatus()` (the same shared request as `refreshCommunityStatus()`, but it rejects, so a failure shows its `formatApiError` detail) and writes each answer back with `setCommunityStatus()`; `lookupCommunityIataLocation()` (`utils/locationPicker.ts`) reads `getCommunitySnapshot()` and asks the backend only before the store knows. Changing the IATA is one `PUT /api/community/me/iata` (no `PATCH` first): the backend clears the old region's status, binds with Community, then saves and reconnects. Joining is that same call with `enable: true` (no `PATCH` either; the settings have no "enable" checkbox, and while Community is on only a "Leave" button sends `PATCH {enabled:false}`; from off, a `PATCH {enabled: true}` is refused with 409 `join_requires_bind`): Community binds the IATA first, and only then does the backend turn Community on; a refused bind (the IATA change cap gives `settings.community.joinIataChangeCap`) leaves it off. The Leave button opens the exit dialog and is described by its help text (`aria-describedby`); the dialog's confirm button reads `settings.community.leaving` while the PATCH runs. `App` derives `directoryEnabled` from the store, so the router leaves `#test` and the test tool disappears as soon as Community is off; `MessageList` passes it to `PathModal`, which then asks `POST /api/directory/resolve-hops` nothing. With Community off, `ObserverReachModal` and `MeshTestView` say so instead of showing an empty list (while waiting for a position they show `CommunityWaitingNotice` instead, with "Choose my position", and `LiveView` shows `settings.community.waiting.liveBanner` with the same button instead of its opt-out banner), the "heard by" badges are hidden, and nothing calls `/api/community/airports` (a lookup in the backend's bundled airport list, no request leaves): the location picker checks the store first and `LiveView` frames the chosen regions only while Community is on. There is no airport search (the FX-Port autocomplete is gone). The IATA form (the join form while off, the IATA field while on or waiting) shows `IataSuggestions`: nothing is asked on display (nor after an opt-out); a "Suggest codes" button (`settings.community.suggest.button`, described by `.help`, which says what leaves) asks `api.suggestCommunityIata()` (`GET /api/community/iata/suggest`, which sends the radio's position rounded to about 1 km to Community, even while off) and then offers up to two buttons, the neighbours' code (`settings.community.suggest.neighbors`) and the nearest airport (`.nearest`), one (`.both`) when they are the same code, in a `role="group"` named by `settings.community.suggest.title`; a click only fills the field (`aria-pressed` follows it), never joins or binds, and any other code can still be typed. No position shows `.none`, a failed request `.failed` (in a polite live region).

## Community live status
`livePacketStore.applyLiveStatus` keeps only what `LiveView` shows: the opt-out flag, `auth_error`, `clock_skew_s`, `banned` (state `banned`: Community banned this radio key, banner `live.bannerBanned` with the button to the Community settings and no retry; the opt-out wins over it, and it wins over a token refusal and a pause), the banner they produce, and `paused`. Close codes never reach the user, so the store ignores `close_code` and `connected`. It reads `state` to treat `auth_rejected` as `token_rejected` for older relays, and `paused` (Community paused its public feed) as a flag that opt-out and a token refusal override. `LiveView` shows a paused feed as a small chip in the toolbar, inside a `role="status"` region that stays mounted empty (`sr-only`) so a new pause is announced (`live.paused`, tooltip `live.pausedHelp` on an `aria-hidden` node, so screen readers get the label and the help once, from `sr-only` text), never a banner, and only while no banner is shown.

## `#live`
`LiveView` + `components/live/` animate packets along their hops over the directory map (`GET /api/directory/nodes/live`). There are **no observer icons**: `NODE_ROLE_STYLE` has no observer role. Observer GPS stays in `geometryDirectoryNodes` only so that hops can be resolved, and `mappableDirectoryNodes` skips it. One observation draws one polyline: origin (from `origin.pubkey` / local advert, never `hops[0]`), then the hops, then the ear. Two or more points draw a line, one point a pulse. A 300 ms hold (`LIVE_HOLD_MS`) merges frames for the same path. `MAX_CONCURRENT_ANIMS` caps how many animations run at once.

Region filter: `LiveViewFilters.iata` is a `Set` (empty = every region), picked in `live/LiveIataMenu.tsx` (a disclosure button named by its visible text, label + selection, opening a `role="group"` of checkboxes with "All regions"; focus goes to the first box on open and back to the button on Escape). Unticking the last region means every region, and so does a list over 64 codes (`LIVE_IATA_SELECTION_MAX`, the relay's limit). Options = `GET /api/community/iata/active` (only while Community is ready; failures are ignored) ∪ the regions seen in the feed ∪ the radio's IATA, the placeholders `XXX`, `LOC`, `AAA` excluded (`LIVE_PLACEHOLDER_IATAS`). Default = the radio's IATA; the choice is kept in localStorage `meshloom-live-iata` (`utils/liveIataPreference.ts`: `"all"` or a JSON list, every access in try/catch; it stays after an opt-out, on purpose, for the return) and sent with the subscribe, every 30 s heartbeat, and at once on change. Opening Live or changing the choice frames the chosen regions: `utils/liveRegionCenters.ts` places each code at its airport (`GET /api/community/airports`, exact IATA match in the backend's bundled list, one lookup per code per page). Only while Community is on: off, the map frames everything and nothing is asked. An empty answer or a failure is not remembered, and the cache is cleared whenever the store turns Community on, so turning it back on frames the regions again without a reload. Each answer's `session_id` replaces the tab's: a relay that forgot the session (TTL, restart) mints a new one, and the next beats reuse it. The filter follows the packet: `LiveMapController` keeps a bounded `LivePacketRegionIndex` (bucket key → regions that heard it, local observations counted under the radio's IATA), and `observationPassesFilters` lets an observation through when its packet was heard in a chosen region. Local observations (`iata: null`) always pass. The backend relay filters upstream too, but `community_packet` reaches every tab, so the browser filter is what decides.
